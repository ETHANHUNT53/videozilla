# F2.3 — Turn an idea into a brief

**Sources:** [M2 design §2, §4](../M2_DESIGN.md) (approved), PRD F2-FR3 / F2-FR11 / §21, API Design §13.3, DB Design §4.4, System Design §8. Built on F2.2's `generateJson` and the shared `ideas` limit.

## What you'll see

A **brief** is the plan for one video: a title, a spoken hook, the angle, the audience, 3–5 key points in teaching order, and what's left out to fit 60–90 seconds.

1. **Two ways to get one:**
   - **Find a topic:** "Use this" on an idea card turns that card into a brief.
   - **I have an idea:** describe it (up to 500 characters) and press **Sharpen my idea**.
   - **Use my notes** stays "arrives in a later update" (F2.4).
2. **The brief card** appears under the tabs, whichever tab is open. It shows the title, hook, angle, audience, key points and "Left out to fit the length".
3. **Two other angles** sit under the brief. **Use this angle** swaps one in at once, with no AI call and nothing counted. The brief you had moves into its place, so you can switch back.
4. **The video's title follows the brief title**, but only while you haven't named the video yourself:
   - it's still the default "Untitled video", or
   - it's still the title an earlier brief gave it.

   A title you typed is never replaced (PRD F2-FR11).
5. **Generate storyboard** shows, disabled, with "// next milestone" (that's F3).
6. **It's all saved with the video:** the brief, the two angles and the title are still there after a reload.

## API: `POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/idea/brief`

**Request body** (strict). `from` says where the brief comes from:

| `from` | Other fields | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `"idea"` | — | Brief from the **saved** "I have an idea" text (`ideaInput`). The page saves pending typing first. |
| `"suggestion"` | `suggestionId` (`sg_…`) | Brief from that saved idea card. |
| `"alternative"` | `index` (0 or 1) | **New in F2.3.** Swaps in one of the two other angles. No AI call, not counted. |
| `"notes"` | — | F2.4. Today: `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `details[0].field = "from"`, message "Briefs from notes arrive in a later update." |

**Why `400` and not `501` for `notes`:** the error catalogue (API Design §7) has no 501 code, and §7 answers request values the server doesn't accept with `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`. When F2.4 makes `notes` work, no client breaks.

**Response:** `200 { brief, alternatives: [2], project }`.
- `brief` is `{ title, hook, angle, audience, keyPoints[3–5], outOfScope[] }`. `audience` is one of `kids` / `students` / `college` / `general`.
- `alternatives` holds 2 more briefs of the same shape, each with a clearly different angle.
- `project` is the `Project` resource, so the page can show a title that changed without a reload. It's an addition to API Design §13.3.

**Saved:** `brief`, `briefAlternatives` (new field, DB Design §4.4) and `briefUpdatedAt` go on the idea session, and the project's "last changed" time moves (M2 D3), all in one transaction.
- The idea's `rev` doesn't change: like suggestions, `rev` guards what you type, so a brief never makes the next autosave look like a conflict.
- A title that follows the brief bumps the project's `rev`, like any rename.

**Errors:**

| When | Answer |
|---|---|
| `from: "idea"` with an empty saved idea | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, field `ideaInput` |
| Unknown `suggestionId`, or one from another video or someone else's (cards are only looked up in this video's idea session) | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, field `suggestionId` (never `404`: the video itself exists) |
| `from: "alternative"` with no angle at that index | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, field `index` |
| The video isn't in the Idea stage (`edit_idea`), before the call or after it | `409 INVALID_STATE`; nothing saved |
| Today's `ideas` limit is reached | `429 LIMIT_REACHED` + `Retry-After` + RateLimit headers; no AI call |
| A prohibited idea (PRD §21.1), or Gemini's own safety filters | `422 UNSAFE_CONTENT`, with our own sentence for the category (as in F2.2), `field: "idea"` |
| Gemini still busy after the retries and the fallback, or no usable reply | `503 AI_BUSY` + `Retry-After` |
| Unknown or someone else's video | `404`, like everywhere |

**Limit:** the AI cases (`idea`, `suggestion`) count one unit of the shared `ideas` limit (150/day), the same way as F2.2:
- counted before the call;
- given back when nothing is delivered (AI busy, an unusable reply, the video moved on or was deleted meanwhile);
- refusals and safety blocks still count.

## AI call (`packages/ai/src/brief.ts`)

- **Call:** `generateJson`, the same models, 15 s deadline, quota circuit breaker and repair round as F2.2. Logged in `ai_calls` with task `brief`.
- **To recheck once briefs run live:** the time budget. A brief (title, hook, angle, audience, 3–5 key points, what's left out, plus 2 other angles) is a bigger reply than 5 idea cards, and the 15 s deadline and the tier budgets (Gemini 11 s, Groq 4–9 s, OpenRouter 6 s; [chain](AI-provider-switch.md#three-tier-chain-added-2026-10-19)) were sized on idea cards. Compare the `latencyMs` of `brief` rows in `ai_calls` with those of `ideas` rows, and raise the brief's deadline (`deadlineMs`) if briefs time out.
- **Inputs:**
  - the idea text, or the card's title, hook, angle and key points;
  - the video's language, length (60 or 90 s), audience and board or exam;
  - the creator's "what I teach or create about".
- **Data, not instructions:** all user text goes inside `<idea>`, `<idea_card>` and `<creator_context>` tags, with angle brackets replaced by look-alikes (‹ ›). The system prompt says never to follow instructions inside them.
- **What the prompt asks for:**
  - **Key points:** 3–4 for 60 s, 4–5 for 90 s.
  - **Out of scope:** 0–3 short items.
  - **A broad idea** is narrowed to one teachable focus, and the rest goes to "out of scope". **A trivial one** is broadened a little (PRD F2 edge cases).
  - **Current events:** no specific figures.
  - **Sensitive topics** stay general education.
  - **Alternatives:** two clearly different angles on the same idea, never the main brief reworded.
- **Refusals:** the same fixed categories as F2.2, shown with the app's own sentences.
- **Trimmed for display:** title 80, hook 200, angle 300, each key point and out-of-scope item 160 characters.

## The page

- **Saving first:** "Sharpen my idea" waits for the autosave to finish (a new `settle()` on the autosave queue), so the server reads exactly what's on screen. If the text can't be saved (offline, another tab changed it), the button says so and doesn't call the AI.
- **One AI request at a time** across the Idea step: suggestions and briefs share the daily limit and the "Thinking…" state, and a second click is ignored (ref guard).
- **The limit lock is seeded on load (QA note 1):** the page reads `GET /api/v1/me/usage`. If today's `ideas` are used up, every AI button starts disabled until `resetsAt`, with the same "reached today's limit" message a `429` shows, so a reload doesn't turn them back on. After a `429` they lock the same way. "Use this angle" stays available, since switching isn't an AI request and isn't counted.
- **Narrow screens:** the brief card's two-column details use a container query, so they stack when the card is narrow (beside the settings panel, or on a phone).
- **Contrast (QA note 2):** the old "Use this arrives in the next update" note is gone. Hints, counters and notes in the Idea step use the muted text colour (about 6:1) instead of the dim one (about 3:1).

## Also in F2.3 (QA notes on F2.2)

- **Refusal copy:** the `illegal` sentence is now "It asks how to do something illegal, or how to make something dangerous."
- **Timing of unsafe topics (documented, split not changed):**
  - What happens: the main model can spend its whole budget (15 s − 4 s kept for the fallback = 11 s) on an unsafe topic before it's aborted. Then the fallback refuses in about 0.8 s. QA saw about 18 s end to end in development (dev compile time included).
  - Why the split stays: capping the main model at about 9 s would hand most ordinary Hindi requests to the weaker fallback, since the main model takes 9–12 s for 5 Hindi idea cards (measured 2026-10-17).
  - It's rare, the answer is still right (a refusal), and the paid tier (System Design Q9) is faster. Revisit the split when we move to the paid tier.
- **The fallback's Hindi (QA note 3):** with the main model's free daily quota used up, live checks run on `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, whose Hindi spellings of technical terms are weaker. That's a model limit, not a prompt bug, and it goes away on the paid tier.

## Also changed: field-level repair messages

Both AI reply schemas (ideas and briefs) were a `z.union` of "refusal" and "result". A union's error is only "(root): Invalid input", so the repair round couldn't tell Gemini what to fix. The new `refusalOr(result)` in `packages/ai/src/ideas.ts` picks the refusal schema when the reply has `refused`, and otherwise reports the result's own field errors (for example `brief.audience`, `brief.keyPoints`).

## Review fixes (after 32ab466)

- **"Use this angle" is idempotent:** the request can carry `briefUpdatedAt` (the brief the user was looking at; the IdeaSession resource and the brief response now include it). If the brief has changed meanwhile (a retried request, a second tab, a double send), nothing is swapped and the current brief comes back, instead of the swap undoing itself. The UI always sends it.
- **`refusalOr`:** only `refused: true` is a refusal. A good result that also carries `"refused": false` parses as the result (the stray key is dropped) instead of costing a repair round or ending in 503.
- Reviewed and fine: the title rule (read and write in one transaction, so a concurrent rename conflicts and is retried; an explicit name is never overwritten); the brief response carries the project so the page takes its new rev; `settle()` resolves for saved, offline, error, conflict and stopped; `index` is 0 or 1 and checked against the stored angles.
- **Hand-over judgment calls 3 and 6** (reviewed after the notes were found):
  - `briefAlternatives` on the idea session is fine: exactly 2 short briefs (display lengths, at most 3 out-of-scope points), replaced by every new brief (`applyBrief` sets brief, alternatives and `briefUpdatedAt` together), so stale angles never outlive their brief; older sessions read it as `[]`.
  - The 4 s fallback reserve stays. Local `ai_calls`: the main model answered idea requests in 6.1–8.6 s (inside its 11 s), the fallback in 2.6–2.9 s (inside its 4 s; about 1 s to spare). There's no successful brief call to measure yet (the key is out of credits), so recheck once briefs run live.
  - **Out of credits (402)** was handled as a passing "busy": three failed calls per request and "try again in 30 s" forever. It's now a quota cooldown: the key's models are skipped for 30 min (answer at once with `Retry-After: 1800`), and the server log says to top up billing in Google AI Studio.
