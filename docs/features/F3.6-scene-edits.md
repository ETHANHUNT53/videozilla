# F3.6 — Manual scene edits (text, delete, reorder)

**Status:** design **approved 2026-10-22 with changes** ([your answers](#your-answers-2026-10-22): the scene type is editable too, any scene down to 1, drag-and-drop, confirm when timing or chapters change). Being built. Where the sections below differ, [What changes with your answers](#what-changes-with-your-answers) wins.

**Sources:** PRD F3-FR7 (light direct edits without chat: narration and on-screen text inline, delete a scene, reorder, "Regenerate this scene"; each edit creates a storyboard version, P1), F3 structure rules (hook first, recap / outro last, 8–40 words a scene, on-screen text limits), F3 edge cases (an edited narration that loses a cue phrase is re-anchored; "Approve" disabled below 3 scenes), §13.2 (scene ids are ours and stable), §13.4 (validation after every change), §14.2 (scene-type limits); API Design §13.4 ("Manual scene edits": `PATCH` / `DELETE` / `move` with `If-Match`, `201 StoryboardVersion`, `400` with the field path); DB Design §4.6 / §6.3 (`source: manual_edit`, `changedSceneIds`); Stitch screen 10 (the edit ✎, regenerate ✦, delete 🗑 and drag ⋮⋮ icons on each card, left out in F3.3); built on F3.1–F3.5 (versions, pointer restore, the review screen, the Instagram panel's Edit → Save pattern).

## What you'll see

1. Each scene card on the Storyboard step gets small actions in its top-right corner (Stitch 10): **Edit** ✎, **Delete** 🗑 and **Move up / Move down** (Q4). Not on an older version opened from the history (read-only, F3.5), and not while a storyboard is being written.
2. **Edit** turns that card into a form (one card at a time, Q5):
   - **Narration**: a text box with a word counter (`23 words · ~9 s`, a hint outside 8–40 words);
   - **On screen**: the scene's **headline(s)** and the **labels of its visual**, each its own one-line field with its limit, depending on the scene type (Q1): the TitleHook headline and subline, the KeywordReveal keywords, the BulletSteps title and items, the Flowchart nodes, the Comparison titles and points, the StatCounter number and label, the RecapOutro points and call to action, the Illustration title and labels, the UnitGrid label and notes;
   - **Save** / **Cancel**. Save makes a new storyboard version (`Edited scene 3`); the preview thumbnail, the timing and the length chip update.
3. **Delete** asks first ("Delete scene 4? You can bring it back from the version history.") and makes a new version (`Deleted scene 4`).
4. **Move up / Move down** swaps the scene with its neighbour and makes a new version (`Moved scene 5 up`).
5. The scene type itself isn't changed here, and **Regenerate this scene** (AI) comes with the revision engine (Q6).

## Rules (one place: `packages/pipeline`, used by the API and shown on the page)

- **Narration:** 1–80 words, no control characters; 8–40 words is the hint (as generated). Only the words change; the scene's type and visual stay.
- **On-screen text:** the scene type's own limits (schema `SCENE_PROPS`: e.g. KeywordReveal ≤ 3 keywords of ≤ 32 characters, BulletSteps items ≤ 70, Flowchart nodes ≤ 32, headline beats ≤ 70); only the text fields are editable (no ids, positions, icons or numbers' structure); counts of items stay as they are (no adding / removing bullets in this feature).
- **Structure:** the **hook stays first** and the **recap / outro stays last**: they can be edited but not moved or deleted; body scenes move only among the body scenes. Delete is allowed down to **3 scenes** (Q3); below the PRD range (6–12 / 8–16) the chips show a warning, and "Approve" (M3) will be disabled below 3.
- **Cues after a narration edit** (Q2): each cue whose anchor words are still in the narration keeps them; one whose words are gone is **re-anchored automatically** to the closest word (the validator's fuzzy match, ≥ 0.8), else it falls back to even spacing in the scene. The card says "Timing adjusted for 2 visuals" when that happens. Headline beats' anchors the same way.
- **`beat` after a move or delete** (Q2): chapters are recomputed with the F3.1 rules: a moved scene **joins the chapter of the scene above it** (so chapters stay together), chapters are renumbered 1, 2, 3… in order, a chapter left without scenes disappears; diagram steps are renumbered in order. `themeHints` (none yet) are left untouched on the scenes they belong to. Moods stay.
- **Scene ids are stable** (`s1`, `s2`, … as generated, §13.2): a move or delete never renames other scenes; cards show their position (S1, S2…) as now.
- **Length:** words and the estimated duration are recomputed on every save (the version's `validationReport`), so the duration chip and the scene timings follow.
- **`changedSceneIds`:** the edited scene; for a move, the scenes whose order changed; for a delete, empty (nothing to rebuild for a removed scene) plus `deletedSceneIds` in the summary. These drive the incremental build later (M3).

## API (API Design §13.4, as designed)

All three need `If-Match: "<currentStoryboardVersion>"` (`412` with the current version when it moved on), only in `storyboard_ready`, on the current version, and answer `201 { version, project }` (a new version, `source: "manual_edit"`, `basedOnVersion` = the current one):

- **`PATCH /api/v1/projects/{id}/storyboard/scenes/{sceneId}`** `{ narration?, props?, beats? }`: `props` may only change text fields of the scene's type (any other change → `400`, `details[].field` like `props.items[2].text`); `beats` only their `headline` text. A save that changes nothing → `200`, no new version.
- **`DELETE /api/v1/projects/{id}/storyboard/scenes/{sceneId}`**: `409 INVALID_STATE` for the hook, the last scene, or when 3 scenes are left.
- **`POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/storyboard/scenes/{sceneId}/move`** `{ afterSceneId: string | null }` (the API design's shape; the page sends the neighbour for up / down): `409` for the hook / last scene or a position outside the body.
- `404` for an unknown scene, version or video (and someone else's). Cross-user cases for all three.
- Not counted against any limit (no AI, Q6).
- `POST …/scenes/{sceneId}/regenerate` (AI) is not built here (Q6).

## The page

- `components/scene-edit.tsx`: the card's actions and its edit form (fields generated from the scene type's text fields; counters and limits from the same rules; Save off with the reasons, like the caption panel).
- Focus: Edit → the narration box; Cancel / Save → back to Edit; after Move, focus stays on the moved card's Move button; after Delete, on the next card's Edit (or the previous one).
- Announced politely: "Scene 4 deleted", "Scene 5 moved up".
- A save on an old pointer (another tab restored or edited) → `412`, the stale-tab line and the latest version (the typed edit is kept in the form, as the caption panel does since F3.4).
- Phone (360 px): the actions are icon buttons with labels for screen readers; the form is full width.

## What changes with your answers

- **Scene type:** the Edit form has a **Type** menu with the 9 types the renderer draws (TitleHook, KeywordReveal, BulletSteps, Flowchart, Comparison, StatCounter, RecapOutro, Illustration, UnitGrid). Each type has its own fields (its props, §14.2, within the schema limits: lists can grow or shrink inside their ranges, e.g. 2–4 bullets). Switching type converts what carries over (texts become the headline / title, list items ↔ bullets / nodes / keywords / points, a number found in the text becomes a StatCounter value) and leaves the rest empty, so Save stays off until the new type is valid. The preview draws the new type.
- **Cues** after any edit are rebuilt for the scene's element ids: a cue whose target still exists and whose words are still in the narration is kept; otherwise it is re-anchored to the closest word (fuzzy ≥ 0.8), else to the element's own words if the narration says them, else left to even spacing at build time. `beat` (chapter, mood, diagram) is kept; `themeHints` untouched.
- **Delete / move:** any scene, down to 1 (the last scene can't be deleted). Deleting the video's only hook or its last recap / outro (wherever it sits after a move) shows a warning in the confirm ("The video will be left without a hook." / "… without a recap.").
- **Chapters after a move:** the moved scene joins the chapter of the scene now above it (or below, at the top); chapters are renumbered 1, 2, 3… in order and a chapter left empty disappears; diagram steps renumbered. The hook / recap keep having no chapter.
- **Confirm (ask each time):** the page works out the change with the same code the server uses (`packages/pipeline`), and when cues are re-timed or chapters change it shows e.g. "2 visuals re-timed in scene 3 · scene 5 moves from chapter 2 to chapter 1 · chapters renumbered", with **Save** / **Cancel**. No confirm when nothing like that changes (a delete still asks "Delete scene 4?").
- **Drag and drop** (no new library): a ⋮⋮ handle on each card; drag with a mouse or a finger (pointer events, the page scrolls near the edges); or focus the handle and press **Space** to pick up, **↑ / ↓** to move, **Space** to drop, **Escape** to cancel; each step announced ("Scene 5, position 4 of 8"). The drop goes through the same confirm and saves one version.
- **Minimums:** manual edits only need 1 scene; the generated storyboard's checks are unchanged. PRD F3-FR7 / edge case and API Design §13.4 updated.


## Built (2026-10-22)

- **Shared logic** (`packages/pipeline/src/scene-edit.ts`): one `applySceneOp` for an edit (narration, headline, type, fields), a delete and a move. The page runs it first to know what will change and ask; the server runs it again inside the save. It also holds the 9 scene types' forms (`SCENE_FORMS`), the type conversion, the checks (`sceneProblems`), cue re-anchoring (`rebuildCues`), chapter / diagram reflow (`reflowBeats`) and the confirm's wording (`describeChanges`).
- **API:** `PATCH /api/v1/projects/{id}/storyboard/scenes/{sceneId}` (narration, type, props, headlines), `DELETE …/scenes/{sceneId}`, `POST …/scenes/{sceneId}/move` `{ afterSceneId }`. Each needs `If-Match: "<current version>"` (`400` without it, `412` with the current version when stale), works only when the storyboard is ready (`409` while generating), saves one new version (`source: manual_edit`, its summary such as "Moved scene 5 to position 6", the changed scene ids, a fresh length report) in a transaction and keeps the newest 30. A change that changes nothing returns `200` with the current version; an invalid one `400` with the field paths; deleting the last scene `409`; another user's video `404`.
- **Storyboard step:** ✎ Edit, 🗑 Delete and the ⋮⋮ drag handle on each card of the current version (none on an old version being viewed, none while a storyboard is being written). Edit opens the scene's form in place (narration with word count, headline, scene type, that type's fields), with Save / Cancel; Save stays off while something is invalid. Before a save that re-times cues or moves chapters, and before every delete, a confirm lists what changes, with Save / Cancel (or Delete / Cancel).
- **Drag and drop** with no new library: mouse or finger (pointer events, the page scrolls near the edges), or the keyboard on the handle (Space picks up, ↑ / ↓ move, Space drops, Escape cancels). Every step is announced to screen readers, and so is each save ("Scene 2 saved.", "Moved scene 5 to position 6.", "Scene 2 deleted.").
- **Focus:** after a save it goes to the scene's Edit button, or its handle after a move; after a delete, to the scene that took its place; Cancel on a confirm returns to the button that opened it.
- **Stale tab:** if another tab changed the storyboard first, the page reloads it and says "This video moved on in another tab, so your scene change wasn't saved."

## QA fixes (2026-10-22)

- **Type switching** converts from the saved scene each time (with what was typed in the saved type's own form), not from the type shown last. A chain such as Bullet steps → Flowchart → Big number → Recap → Title hook keeps every item. Switching back to a type shows what was typed there. A Big number with no number in the scene is left empty: there's no "0" any more, so none carries into later types. Narration and headlines are shared by every type and stay as typed.
- **412 keeps the edit:** if another tab changed the storyboard first, the typed edit is kept in this tab (sessionStorage, per video and scene). The form opens again on the newer version with a note: "The storyboard changed (another tab or a regenerate), so your edit wasn't saved. It's back in the form…". Only that note shows (no second page line), as in the caption panel. A 409 (a new storyboard being written) doesn't keep it, since those scenes are new.
- **One length estimate:** the form's "~7s" is the card's own estimate for the typed words (the same timeline), not a separate words ÷ 150 sum.
- **Touch targets:** Edit, Delete and the drag handle are 44 × 44.
- **Plain problems:** the form uses its own labels and plain wording, e.g. "Call to action is empty.", "Number is empty.", "Steps needs at least 2." (and the API's `400` details read the same way).
- **Move confirm:** while it asks, the cards show the new order's numbers and times; Cancel puts them back.
