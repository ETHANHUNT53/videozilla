# F3.7 — Accuracy check flags

**Status:** design **approved 2026-10-22** ([answers](#your-answers-2026-10-22)); building.

**Sources:**
- PRD F3-FR10: after generation, a lightweight AI self-review flags claims it is unsure about, as a scene-level warning icon with an explanation; the user can ask for a fix in chat or dismiss it. P1.
- PRD §13.1 T5: `llm.fast`, an `AccuracyReport` JSON with per-scene flags, ≤ 10 s, async and non-blocking.
- PRD §16.1: `accuracy_check` is the last step of `storyboard.generate`, after `save_version`.
- PRD §21.3: no invented statistics, quotes or dates; the check flags uncertain claims; a subtle note on the storyboard screen says the user is responsible for checking facts before posting.
- PRD F10-FR10: optional steps such as the accuracy check never block the pipeline; on failure they fall back and log.
- PRD R2 (inaccurate facts).
- DB Design §4.6: `accuracyReport` is the only field of a version that may be filled in later. §4.10: `ai_calls.task: "accuracy"`.
- API Design: `StoryboardVersion.accuracyFlags: { sceneId, claim, note }[]` (today always `[]`).
- Builds on F3.1–F3.6: the job and its steps, the review screen's scene cards, versions, and manual edits.

## What you'll see

1. **After a generate or regenerate**, the storyboard shows as soon as it is saved, as now. A small chip in the chips row says **"Checking facts…"** for a few seconds. When the check is done it becomes **"2 claims to check"**, or **"Facts checked ✓"** when nothing was flagged.
2. **On a flagged scene card**, a **⚠ icon** sits next to the scene's label ("S4 · EXAMPLE ⚠"). The card shows a short orange box:
   > **Check this:** "Light travels 300,000 km per second in water"
   > Light is about 25 % slower in water (about 225,000 km/s); 300,000 km/s is its speed in a vacuum.
   >
   > [Edit scene] [Dismiss]
   - **Edit scene** opens the F3.6 edit form on that scene. "Fix in chat" comes with the revision chat in M3 (F4); the button will switch to it then.
   - **Dismiss** hides the flag ("I checked it"). The chip counts only open flags; a "Show dismissed (1)" link brings dismissed flags back.
3. **The PRD's note**, small and below the chips: *"AI can get facts wrong. Check names, numbers and dates before you post."* It always shows on the Storyboard step, flagged or not.
4. **If the check fails** (AI busy, timeout, bad output), nothing blocks: the chip says **"Facts not checked · Check now"**. The video can still be approved, built and posted.
5. **Check now / Check again** (in the chip's menu) runs the check on demand, for example after manual edits.

## How it works

- **The check (T5):** one `llm.fast` call through the same chain as the other tasks (Gemini → Groq → OpenRouter, or the mock).
  - **Input:** the video's language, its title, and each scene's id, narration and on-screen text (no notes, chat or personal data).
  - **Output:** `AccuracyReport { flags: [{ sceneId, claim, note, kind }] }`, checked with Zod. `claim` is quoted from the scene; `note` is one or two sentences saying why it's doubtful and what is more likely right; `kind` is `number | date | name | quote | fact`.
  - **Limits:** at most 2 flags per scene and 6 per video; a flag whose claim isn't actually in that scene's text is dropped (no invented quotes).
  - **Prompt:** conservative. Flag only checkable factual claims that are likely wrong or that a careful teacher would want to verify (numbers, dates, names, quotes, "the largest / first / only"). Never flag opinions, analogies, simplifications that are fine for the audience, or style. Notes and pasted text stay delimited data (§21.4).
- **When it runs:**
  - automatically after `save_version` in the generate job, as the step `check_facts`. The run is already "saved" and the storyboard shows; the check fills in `accuracyReport` on that version and never fails the run;
  - on demand with **Check now** (see Q1 and Q2).
- **Storage:** `storyboard_versions.accuracyReport = { status: "pending" | "done" | "failed", flags: [{ id, sceneId, claim, note, kind }], dismissed: [flagId], checkedAt, model, provider }`. This is the one field DB §4.6 allows to change on a saved version, so dismissing or checking doesn't make a new version.
- **New versions** (manual edit, caption edit, restore, later revisions):
  - flags on scenes whose narration and on-screen text are unchanged carry over, dismissed or not;
  - flags on changed or deleted scenes are dropped, since the claim may be gone;
  - Q1 decides whether changed scenes are re-checked automatically.
- **API:**
  - `StoryboardVersion` gains `accuracy: { status: "none" | "pending" | "done" | "failed", checkedAt, openCount }`.
  - `accuracyFlags` becomes `{ id, sceneId, claim, note, kind, dismissed }[]`.
  - `POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/storyboard/accuracy-check` (If-Match, `202`) starts a check on the current version.
  - `POST …/storyboard/accuracy-flags/{flagId}/dismiss` and `…/restore` (If-Match, `200`) dismiss a flag or bring it back.
  - `GET …/status` already polls while the run is active; the page also polls the version while `accuracy.status` is `pending`.
- **Jobs:** the check is a step of `storyboard.generate` (Inngest `step.run`, or inline when Inngest is off). On demand, a small job `storyboard.accuracy` does the same, one at a time per video. Neither blocks Approve or a build.
- **Logging:** each call goes to `ai_calls` with `task: "accuracy"`.
- **Mocks for tests (no real AI):** the mock provider flags the first scene whose narration has a number. A `[mock:facts-busy]` title makes it fail, and `[mock:facts-none]` makes it return no flags. Tests and the browser check use only these.

## What isn't in this feature

- Web search or sources. The check uses only the model's own knowledge, so a flag means "worth checking", not "proven wrong". Grounded checking with sources could come later.
- Automatic fixing, and "Fix in chat". The chat is M3; until then the fix is a manual edit.
- Checks on the idea brief. Only the storyboard is checked.

## What changes with your answers

- **Notes in English** for every video (Q6). The prompt says so, and the claim must be a quote from the scene's own text, in its own script.
- **Check now** costs 1 revision from the 100 / day (Q2). It is charged when the check starts and refunded if the check fails (AI busy, timeout, bad output). The automatic check after a generate costs nothing.
- **After a manual edit** (Q1): flags on unchanged scenes, dismissed or not, carry to the new version; edited or deleted scenes have none, and the chip says "Scene 3 changed · Check now". The same goes for caption edits (no scene changes, so every flag carries) and restores (the restored version keeps its own report).
- Everything else is as designed above.
