## What you'll see

The Storyboard step of a video (after **Generate storyboard**) becomes Stitch 10:

1. **Header** (F3-FR2): ← library, the video title, the status chip and the stepper, as on the Idea step. Under it a row of chips: language · `~60s · est. 58s` · `8 scenes` · voice · theme (`Midnight`) · `Version 3`, and on the right **Approve & create video** (see Q4).
2. **Duration guard** (F3-FR9): when the estimate is outside the 55–65 s / 80–95 s window, the duration chip turns orange with "a little long / short" and a **Regenerate** link to the Idea step. (The one-click "Shorten to ~60s" needs the revision engine, M5.)
3. **Scene cards** (F3-FR3), in order, grouped under their chapter headings (`// chapter 1 · Light`, from `beat`):
   - left: a **9:16 thumbnail** of the scene in the video's theme, with its time range (`00:07 – 00:18`) on top; it shows a still frame and **plays the scene's animation** on hover, keyboard focus or tap (Q2);
   - right: `S2 · EXPLAIN` (number and role), the scene-type chip (`Flowchart`), `~11s`, the **narration**, and a mono `// on screen:` line with the scene's on-screen text (its headline beats and the labels in its props, e.g. "Cut → Gas → Acid → Tears").
4. **Below the scenes:** the Instagram caption and hashtags, read-only, as on today's list (F3.4 makes them a proper panel; Q3).
5. **Right column** (380 px on desktop, under the scenes on a phone): the settings panel as on the Idea step, and a "// revise with ai — arrives in M5" card in Stitch's place for the chat (Q3).
6. **Back to idea** stays (stepper "open", or a link), and so does **Regenerate storyboard** there; the F3.1 progress card and failure line work as now.

Not in this feature (later rows): scene edit / regenerate / delete / drag icons (F3.6), "check fact" badges (F3.7), the version dropdown (F3.5: a plain `Version N` chip now), the revision chat and "Editing: Scene 3" selection (M5, F7), the caption copy button and editing (F3.4), streaming "4 of 7 generated" (V1 has no streaming).

## Where it lives

The review screen **is the project page's Storyboard step** (Q1): the same URL, header and stepper as the Idea step; the F3.1 "storyboard ready" summary card is replaced by it. `/projects/{id}/storyboard` (F3.1's simple list) redirects to `/projects/{id}`, so old links keep working. "Next: Storyboard →" in the header is replaced on this step by **Approve & create video**.

## Thumbnails (in the browser, no server rendering)

- **Placeholder timing** (PRD F3-FR3): a new `estimateTimeline(storyboard, styleId)` in `packages/pipeline` makes the timeline the real one will look like, without audio: each scene's words spread evenly at the language's speaking rate (EN 150 / HI 140 wpm, as the validator estimates) + the usual lead-in and tail; it reuses `buildTimeline`, so cues land on their anchor words exactly as they will after the voice is recorded. Scene time ranges and `~11s` come from the same estimate.
- **Rendering:** each card renders its scene alone (a one-scene composition, 1080×1920 scaled to the card) with the M0 scene components through `@remotion/player`:
  - a **still frame** by default (Remotion's `<Thumbnail>`, the frame where the scene's visuals are all in: its last cue + ~0.5 s), so 13 cards don't run 13 animations at once on a phone;
  - **plays the scene (looped, muted)** on hover or keyboard focus, or a tap on the ▶ on touch screens; only **one card plays at a time**;
  - `prefers-reduced-motion`: stills only, with a ▶ button.
- **Performance:** the player and scene code load only on this step (`next/dynamic`, client-only), and a card's thumbnail mounts when it scrolls near the screen (IntersectionObserver), with a dark placeholder of the right size before that. Target: the page usable within ~1 s of the data on a mid-range phone; checked with 13 cards at 360 px.
- **Theme (swappable):** thumbnails use the video's `styleId` through `getStyle()` (Midnight today; an unknown id falls back to Midnight). When M3 adds the theme registry, the same call returns the registered theme, so nothing here changes. Captions are off in thumbnails (no voice yet).
- **Fonts:** the scene components' Google fonts (Geist, Noto Sans Devanagari) load as in M0, so Hindi scenes look right.

## Data

- The page already loads the current version on the server (F3.1); it now passes the whole storyboard (≤ 100 KB) instead of the summary. No new API: `GET …/storyboard` already returns it for later client refreshes.
- The `// on screen:` text is derived by a small pure function per scene type (`TitleHook` headline + subline, `KeywordReveal` keywords, `BulletSteps` / `Flowchart` / `RecapOutro` items with `→` or `·`, `Comparison` "A vs B", `StatCounter` value + label, `Illustration` labels, `UnitGrid` notes), plus the headline beats without their `*accent*` marks.

## Packages

- `apps/web` gains `@remotion/player` and `@videozilla/video` (both already in the repo at 4.0.536 / workspace; the lock file changes only in the web app's dependency list).
- `packages/video` exports a `SceneThumbnail` composition (one scene, no audio, no captions) so the web app never reaches into its internals.

## Accessibility

- Each card is a list item with a heading (`Scene 3, Explain, Flowchart, about 11 seconds`); the thumbnail is decorative for screen readers (`aria-hidden`), and the "on screen" line carries its words.
- The play button is a real button with a label ("Play scene 3 preview"); focus shows the same ring as elsewhere.

## Built (2026-10-21)

As designed, with your answers:

- **Where:** the project page's Storyboard step is the review screen once the storyboard is there (while it's written, the F3.1 progress card). `/projects/{id}/storyboard` redirects to the project page. On the Idea step, **Next: Storyboard →** goes back to it.
- **Header:** on the review, **Approve & create video →** is shown disabled with "// Building the video arrives in M3" (under the button on narrow screens, beside it from 1280 px).
- **Chips row:** language · `~60s · est. 59s` (orange with "a little long / short" and a **Regenerate it from the Idea step** line when outside 55–65 s / 80–95 s) · scenes · voice · theme · `Version N`.
- **Scene cards** by chapter (`// chapter 1 · …` from `beat`): `S2 · EXPLAIN`, the scene type, `~7s`, the time range, the narration and `// on screen:` (headline beats + labels in the props, `pipeline/onScreenText`).
- **Previews:** a 9:16 preview per card from the M0 scene components (`@remotion/player`, the whole player and scene code loaded only on this step), timed by `pipeline/estimateTimeline` (words at 150 / 140 wpm through the real timeline builder). A still frame by default; hovering with a mouse plays the scene in a loop with no sound, the ▶ button plays / pauses it (a tap on a phone, or the keyboard); one card at a time; with reduced motion only the button plays. A card's preview mounts when it comes near the screen. The theme is the video's `styleId` through the video package's `getStyle` (Midnight; unknown ids fall back to Midnight), so M3's registry slots in.
- **Placeholders:** the read-only caption and hashtags (with the 2,200 counter) under the scenes; the settings panel and a "// revise with ai … Arrives in M5" card on the right. No edit / delete / drag icons, fact check or version menu.
- **Layout:** two columns from 1280 px (the cards need the width; 1024 px gave cramped text), one column below; the F3.1 failure line and stale-tab line work here too.
- **Packages:** `pipeline/estimate.ts` (`estimateTimeline`, `sceneTimeline`, `stillFrame`, `clock`, `onScreenText`); web gains `@remotion/player`, `remotion`, `@videozilla/video` (already in the lock file).
- **Tests:** 20 new (864 in total), no AI: the estimate (en / hi, back-to-back ranges, one-scene timelines, the still frame), `onScreenText` for every scene type, the review model (labels, chapters, ranges, the duration guard for 60 / 90 s, an unknown theme), and the screen (chips, chapters, cards in order, 8 "on screen" lines, play buttons, caption, Approve disabled with its note, the M5 card, the guard, a failed regenerate, loading).
- **Checked in the browser** on an isolated copy (port 3110, its own database, mock AI), desktop and 360 px: previews draw the scenes in Midnight, hover plays one card at a time, a tap on ▶ plays on a phone, no sideways scrolling, `/storyboard` redirects, Next: Storyboard from the Idea step. Fixed during the check: the ▶ sat under the preview instead of on it, the Approve note squeezed the title at 1024 px, the cards were cramped beside the settings at 1024 px, and a tap on ▶ played and paused at once (hover now only for a real mouse). In development the previews take a few seconds to appear the first time (compiling and fonts).

### Try it live (you)

1. Open a video with a storyboard (or generate one): the Storyboard step shows the chips, the chapters and a card per scene; hover a preview (or tap ▶ on a phone) and the scene animates; hover another and the first stops.
2. Make a Hindi ~90 s storyboard: the previews show Devanagari text, the chips say हिंदी and `~90s`, and if the estimate is outside 80–95 s the length chip turns orange.
