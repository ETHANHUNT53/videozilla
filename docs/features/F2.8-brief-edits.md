# F2.8 — Edit the brief directly

**Status:** design, waiting for your answers (questions at the end). Nothing is built yet.

**Sources:** PRD F2-FR7 (*"Users can directly edit brief fields (title, key points) as plain text, without chat"*, P1) and F2-FR11 (the video title follows the brief title until you rename it); [M2 design §2](../M2_DESIGN.md) (row 6: `PATCH …/idea/brief`); API Design §13.3 (`PATCH /projects/{id}/idea/brief`, partial brief → `200 IdeaSession`); DB Design §4.4. Built on F2.3–F2.6b: the brief card, its 2 other angles, the audience pin, `briefLanguage` / `briefLengthSec`, the `briefUpdatedAt` guards, the title rule.

**Why F2.8 now:** it's next in the approved M2 order (F2.5 → F2.8 → F2.7). F2.7 (the full Stitch screen 09 layout) comes after it, since it lays out the pieces F2.8 adds.

## What you'll see

1. **Edit** on the brief card (next to its title).
2. The card turns into a form with the brief's text fields (Q1): title, hook, angle, key points, "Left out to fit the length".
   - **Key points** as a list: edit each line, **Add a point**, remove one, move one up or down (they're in teaching order).
   - Character counters with the same limits as the card shows (title 80, hook 200, angle 300, each point 160).
3. **Save** (Q2) puts the edited brief on the card at once; **Cancel** drops the edits. No AI call, nothing counted.
4. **The video's title follows an edited brief title** while you haven't named the video yourself (the F2.3 rule, PRD F2-FR11). A title you typed is never replaced.
5. **If the brief changed meanwhile** (a chat answer, "Use this angle", Regenerate, another tab), Save doesn't overwrite it: a bar says *"The brief changed while you were editing."* with **Load latest** (drop my edits) and **Keep mine** (save my edits over it).
6. **While the form is open**, the AI buttons that would replace the brief (chat, Use this angle, Regenerate, a new brief) are paused, so your typing isn't replaced under you; they come back on Save or Cancel.
7. The audience isn't in the form: it's the video's setting (settings panel), as decided in F2.5.

## API: `PATCH /api/v1/projects/{id}/idea/brief`

- **Body** (strict), any of: `title`, `hook`, `angle`, `keyPoints`, `outOfScope`; plus **`briefUpdatedAt`** (required): the brief the edits were made on.
  - Limits, in characters after trimming, no control characters: title 3–80; hook 3–200; angle 3–300; `keyPoints` 1–5 items (Q3) of 2–160; `outOfScope` 0–5 items of 2–160. Fields left out stay as they are.
  - Not editable: `audience` (the video's setting), `sourceNotesSummary` (it describes the notes). Sending them → `400`.
- **Response `200`:** the `IdeaSession` (API Design §13.3) plus `project` (the title may have followed), like other brief answers.
- **Guards:**
  - no brief → `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` (field `brief`);
  - `briefUpdatedAt` isn't the current brief's → `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED` with the current idea in `details.current`, nothing saved;
  - outside the Idea stage (`edit_idea`) → `409 INVALID_STATE`;
  - unknown or someone else's video → `404`.
- **No change** (the edits equal the brief) → `200` with nothing rewritten (`briefUpdatedAt` stays, so another tab's pending chat answer isn't failed for nothing).
- **No AI call, not counted** against the `ideas` limit.

## How it fits with what's there

| Piece | With a direct edit |
|---|---|
| `briefUpdatedAt` guards (chat answers, Regenerate, angle switch) | An edit moves `briefUpdatedAt`, so an AI answer that was based on the older brief gets its usual `412` instead of undoing the edit. The edit itself is guarded the same way (`412` above). |
| Chat | The next chat message works on the edited brief (it's the brief now). The "changed" highlight from the last chat answer goes away on Save. |
| Audience pin | Unchanged: every save sets the brief's audience to the video's setting; the form doesn't show it. |
| `briefLanguage` / `briefLengthSec` | Kept as they were: an edit doesn't rewrite the brief for other settings, so the "Regenerate brief in Hindi?" bar shows exactly as before. |
| The 2 other angles | Kept as they were. "Use this angle" still swaps; the edited brief moves into the angles. |
| Title rule (F2-FR11) | The same check as every brief: the video's title follows the new brief title only while it's still the default or the previous brief's title. |
| A notes brief | Its notes summary stays (not editable); up to 5 "Left out" items, as for notes briefs. |

All of this happens in one transaction (`applyBrief` with the guard), like the other brief saves.

## The page (`components/idea-step.tsx`)

- **Edit / Save / Cancel** on the brief card; the form uses the existing input styles and counters; keyboard friendly (each point is a labelled input; the move buttons say which point they move).
- **Conflict bar** like F2.1's ("Load latest" / "Keep mine"), at the top of the card.
- **Leaving with unsaved edits** shows the browser's "Leave site?" warning, like the idea inputs.
- Read-only outside the Idea stage (no Edit button).

## Tests (no AI involved; throwaway MongoDB)

- **Endpoint:** each field edited and trimmed; partial edits keep the rest; limits (Hindi and emoji counted as characters); `keyPoints` 1–5 and order kept; `audience` / `sourceNotesSummary` / unknown fields → `400`; no brief → `400`; stale `briefUpdatedAt` → `412` with `details.current`; a no-op → nothing rewritten; `409`; `404`; `405` for other methods on the route left as they are.
- **Interplay:** the title follows (default or previous brief title) and never replaces a typed title; audience pinned; `briefLanguage` / `briefLengthSec` kept; angles kept; a chat answer based on the brief before the edit → `412` (the edit isn't undone); no `ideas` counted, no `ai_calls` row.
- **Cross-user** (F1.14 suite and its coverage check): `PATCH` on A's video → `404`, nothing changed.
- **Page helpers:** the key-point list editing (add, remove, move) as a small pure function with unit tests.

## Questions for you

1. **Which fields can be edited:** **all the brief's text** — title, hook, angle, key points and "Left out" (recommended: the same form for everything you see on the card, and the hook is the first sentence the narrator says). Or only **title and key points**, as the PRD's minimum?
2. **How edits are saved:** **Edit → Save / Cancel** (recommended: the brief is the AI's draft and an edit is a deliberate change; it also keeps chat answers from landing in the middle of your typing). Or **autosave as you type**, like the idea text boxes?
3. **Number of key points:** **1 to 5, your choice** (recommended: the limit the database already has; the storyboard step later fits the script to the length). Or keep the AI's rule (3–4 for 60 s, 4–5 for 90 s) for edits too?
