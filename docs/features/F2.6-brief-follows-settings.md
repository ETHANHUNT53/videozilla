# F2.6 follow-up — the brief follows a language (and length) change

## What you'll see

1. **You change the video's language** in the settings panel (for example English → Hindi) while a brief exists. The panel saves as today.
2. **A bar appears on the brief card:** *"This brief is in English; the video is now in Hindi."* with **Regenerate brief in Hindi** and **Keep as is**.
   - It also shows after a reload, as long as the brief and the setting differ, so the choice isn't lost.
   - **Keep as is** hides the bar on this page; it comes back after a reload while the brief still differs (Q4).
3. **Regenerate brief in Hindi:** "Thinking…", then the brief **and its 2 other angles** come back in Hindi, saying the same thing (the same focus, key points, what's left out; chat edits kept), and the bar goes away. Nothing else changes.
4. **The same for a length change** (60 ↔ 90 s), if you say yes to Q2: *"This brief is for about 60 seconds; the video is now 90."* → **Update the brief for 90 seconds** (key points 3–4 ↔ 4–5). Both at once read *"Regenerate brief in Hindi for 90 seconds"*.
5. The usual rules stay: one AI request at a time, the daily limit lock, the busy / unavailable / refusal messages, the title following the brief while you haven't named the video.

## API

**`POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/idea/brief` with `{ "from": "settings" }`** (a new source on the existing endpoint):

- **What it does:** rewrites the **current brief and its 2 other angles** for the video's current language (and length, Q2), keeping their content (Q1). The audience stays the setting, as every brief does now.
- **Needs** a brief, and a brief made for other settings than the video's now: otherwise `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, field `brief` ("The brief already matches the video's settings.").
- **Response:** as every brief: `200 { brief, alternatives: [2], briefUpdatedAt, project }`.
- **Limit:** one `ideas` unit, given back when nothing is delivered (Q3). Refusals and safety blocks count, as before (unlikely here: the content was accepted once).
- **Errors:** as F2.3: `409` outside the Idea stage (before and after the call), `422`, `429`, `503`, `404`.
- **`IdeaSession` resource:** gains `briefLanguage` and `briefLengthSec` (what the brief was made for), so the page can tell when the settings differ, also after a reload.

## Storage (`idea_sessions`, DB Design §4.4)

- **New fields:** `briefLanguage` (`en` | `hi`) and `briefLengthSec` (60 | 90), the settings the brief was written for.
- **Set when a brief is saved** (`applyBrief`), from the video's settings read in the same transaction:
  - a new brief (idea, card, notes), a chat answer (it rewrites the whole brief in the video's language) and a `from: "settings"` rewrite: the current settings;
  - **"Use this angle":** kept as they were, because the angles were written together with the brief.
- **Briefs from before this change** have no `briefLanguage`: they're treated as written for the current settings (no bar). A brief made earlier in another language than the setting won't show the bar until it's remade. No migration.

## AI call (`packages/ai/src/brief.ts`)

- **New source** `{ kind: "rewrite", brief, alternatives, fromLanguage, fromLengthSec }` for `briefIdea`, with its own short rules:
  - write the brief and both angles **in the new language**, saying the same thing (no new focus, no new facts); keep the title's meaning;
  - for a length change: 3–4 key points for 60 s, 4–5 for 90 s, keeping the existing ones where they fit (dropped ones move to "Left out"; new ones come from what's already "Left out" or the angle);
  - a notes brief keeps its notes summary, rewritten in the new language; the notes themselves aren't sent (the brief already says what's in them).
- **Data, not instructions:** the briefs go inside `<brief>` / `<alternative>` blocks with `asData`, like everywhere else.
- **Deadline:** 15 s (three short briefs).
- **Mock mode:** a sample rewrite: the same fields with `[Mock]` and the language noted in the hook; the triggers work.

## The page

- **The bar** sits at the top of the brief card while `briefLanguage` / `briefLengthSec` differ from the video's settings and the Idea stage allows edits; read-only otherwise.
- **The settings panel's message** after such a save says *"Saved. The brief is still in English — you can regenerate it below."* (instead of plain "Saved.").
- **Regenerate** goes through the shared AI runner (one request at a time, limit lock, error line under the bar).
